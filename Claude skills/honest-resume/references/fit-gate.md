# Fit gate

The aim is a verdict that a good recruiter would agree with. A "stretch" that is really a "skip" wastes an application, and sometimes a referral.

Read the person's `career/preferences.md` first. Their rules come before the defaults below.

## Hard gates (if any one fails, the verdict is Skip)

| Gate | Fails when |
| --- | --- |
| Location | The role needs a move or an office they ruled out, or work authorisation they do not have. "Remote" restricted to another country counts as a fail. |
| Pay | A posted range, or a level that typically pays, below their floor. If pay is unknown, the gate passes; flag it as a question for the recruiter. |
| Seniority, too low | A clear step down from their current level, unless their preferences allow it. |
| Seniority, too high | Experience required is more than about 4 years above theirs, or a scope they have never held (profit-and-loss ownership, managing managers at scale, an executive title). |
| Core skill | A must-have is a deep specialism with no `OK` fact behind it (for example a licence, a PhD, or a platform that is the centre of the job). |

## Soft score (roles that pass the gates)

Score each factor 0, 1 or 2 and add them up (maximum 12).

| Factor | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Must-have coverage | 80% or more have an `OK` or `PRIVATE` fact | 50 to 80% | under 50% |
| Years | at or below theirs, or "or equivalent" | 1 to 3 years above | more than 3 above |
| Level | matches their target titles | one step off | further off |
| Core of the work | matches what they want to do every day | partly | barely |
| Domain or industry | theirs | adjacent | unrelated |
| Their stated priority (from preferences, e.g. hands-on, mission, team quality) | strong | some | none |

- 9 to 12: **Apply**
- 6 to 8: **Apply as a stretch.** Name the gaps.
- 0 to 5: **Skip**

## Pay estimates

When no range is posted, estimate from what you know or can look up (salary sites, levels data, the company type and region), and **label it as an estimate**. Never present a guess as fact. A sideways move at similar pay and level is worth pointing out.

## fit-report.md format

```markdown
# <Company> · <Role>

**Verdict:** Apply / Apply as a stretch / Skip (score N/12)
**In one line:** <why, plainly>

## Hard gates
- Location: pass / fail, <evidence>
- Pay: pass / fail / unknown, <posted range, or an estimate labelled as one>
- Seniority: pass / fail, <evidence>
- Core skill: pass / fail, <evidence>

## Requirement coverage
| Must-have | Evidence (fact IDs) | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ... | W-02, W-05 | Strong / Partial / Gap |

## Gaps a recruiter will see
- <gap>: <how to handle it honestly: reframe it, raise it in a cover note, or prepare an interview answer>

## Facts to confirm
- <CONFIRM item that would help, and the exact question to ask>

## Angle
<one line>
```

Keep it short enough to read in a minute.
