# Writing rules

## Voice

- Plain, specific and confident. Avoid "results-driven", "passionate", "spearheaded", "synergy", "cutting-edge" and "leveraged".
- Each bullet says what they did, how, and what changed. Start with a strong verb.
- One idea per bullet, at most two printed lines.
- Numbers only from the facts file, with the same meaning. Never round up, never turn a plan or projection into a result, and never turn "designed for" into "serves".
- No first person, and no full stops at the end of bullets.

## Structure (default; reorder for the role)

1. **Name and contact:** city and remote preference, phone, email, LinkedIn, and a portfolio or GitHub link if relevant.
2. **Headline:** a 6 to 10 word positioning line matched to the role.
3. **Summary:** 2 or 3 sentences: experience, what they lead or do now, and the one proof that matters most for this job.
4. **Proof cards (optional):** two or three short headline facts, such as a before-and-after result, a scale figure or a team size. Use them only when they have strong numbers; drop the section otherwise.
5. **Experience:** most recent employer first. When someone was promoted within one employer, stack the titles under a single block with their dates. The recent, relevant role gets most of the space; older roles get one or two lines, or a single "Earlier career" block.
6. **Skills:** grouped, only what is in the facts file, ordered by the job's priorities.
7. **Education and certifications.**
8. **Projects or portfolio**, when they show something the job values.

## Choosing the angle

Ask what the hiring manager is most worried about, then lead with the fact that answers it. For example:
- **Leadership roles:** team scope, the methods they introduced, results through others.
- **Architect or builder roles:** systems designed and built, technical decisions, scale.
- **Domain roles:** depth in the industry and its constraints.
- **Client-facing or pre-sales roles:** discovery, solutioning, stakeholder outcomes.

## ATS safety

- All text is real, selectable text, with one sensible reading order. Check the extracted text the render script prints.
- Use standard headings: Summary, Experience, Skills, Education, Certifications.
- Don't spread letters out in headings (an ATS reads "S U M M A R Y"). Don't put text in images, use icons that carry meaning, or put contact details in page headers or footers.
- Write dates like "Jun 2024 – Present".
- Name the file `<Full Name> - <Company> - <Role>.pdf`.
